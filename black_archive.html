01 / THE DISPLACED MESSAGE

A shift is not a destination. It is a distance.

03 / THE MARGIN INDEX

The archive's copyist made a mistake. Or so the report claims.

R — Read the first mark. E — Examine the line. A — Attend to the silence. D — Decode the margin. T — Take note of the break. H — Hold the order. E — Extract the initials. G — Gather the marks. A — Arrange nothing. P — Preserve the sequence. S — Submit the instruction.

Take the first mark of each line. The blank lines divide the result into three words. What instruction appears?

HINT 03-A
The first letters are not random. Read them from top to bottom, preserving the two blank-line breaks.
HINT 03-B
The result is a three-word instruction. Keep the spaces where the blank lines separate the groups.

04 / THE BROKEN INDEX

A list is a map when the order is intentional.

06 / E 02 / R 05 / A 01 / O 04 / L 03 / C

Reorder the entries by their numbers. Read the letters. Submit the resulting six-letter word.

HINT 04-A
Ignore the order in which the lines are printed.
HINT 04-B
Sort 01 through 06, then concatenate the letters.

Decrypt the ciphertext using the Vigenère cipher and the supplied key. Submit the plaintext.

HINT 05-A

HINT 05-B
The plaintext is a well-known five-word phrase in cryptography. This particular ciphertext decodes to a short phrase.

The answer is not hidden in the sentence. It is hidden in its shape.

A silent archive keeps its own counsel. Every witness leaves a trace. Never mistake noise for evidence. The final key was never written. Only the patient will notice.

Take the first letter of each line. Then reverse the resulting five-letter sequence. Submit it.

HINT 06-A

HINT 06-B
Reverse those letters. Do not rearrange them in any other way.
